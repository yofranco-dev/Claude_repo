# Trend bot backtest: BTCUSDT_1d

Rule: hold 100% of equity in the coin when the daily close is above its 200-session SMA, otherwise hold cash. Decisions are made at the close and filled at the next session's open. Costs are 0.10% fee and 0.05% slippage per side. The rule was fixed before testing and has no tuned parameters. The holdout period starts 2024-11-22.
