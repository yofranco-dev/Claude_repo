**Takeaway:** a tight take-profit with a wide stop makes win rate easy to push past 70% (some rows exceed 85%), but each loss is several times bigger than each win. After about 0.3% round-trip costs, profit per trade is near zero or negative. Win rate alone is not a measure of a profitable strategy; check profit factor and drawdown.
